# Week 2 — Collections + hashing (25–31 October)

**Roadmap source:** [Hilti Technical Learning Roadmap — 19 Weeks](https://app.notion.com/p/3fd2548df0078107b67ed8a3389cc999).

**Classification:** supplementary Java/DSA practice based on the 19-week roadmap. The individual Week 2 Notion page is blank, so these tutorials are newly authored rather than migrated university assignments. They are not official WIX1002 work.

## Roadmap objectives

**Java:** `ArrayList`, `LinkedList`, `HashMap`, `HashSet`, generics, iterators, `Comparator` / `Comparable`, mutable versus immutable objects.

**DSA:** hashing, frequency counting, duplicates, two pointers, and introductory sliding window.

**Mastery checkpoint:** choose appropriate structures and independently solve basic frequency-counting and array/string problems with complexity analysis.


## How to work through the lessons

Attempt each milestone in a scratch file, then check the stated cases or trace against your actual output. Keep the first attempt and the bug you found. If you use AI for feedback, ask for a hint about your next step or a review of your reasoning rather than a complete solution.

1. [Tutorial 01 — Keep a student roster](Tutorials/Tutorial01/README.md): lists, generics, iteration, and operation costs.
